## What I'm building

The projects are probably going to be all over the place. That's part of the fun. One week it might be a Raspberry Pi and some LEDs, the next it's an AI-powered fantasy football experiment, figuring out how to make my home setup work from a vacation rental, or finding out whether a 12GB graphics card and a Raspberry Pi can run a language model well enough to be worth the trouble.

The common thread? I had a problem, an idea, or an excuse to build something—and AI made it a lot easier to see where it could go. Something usually broke along the way. That part's in here too.
